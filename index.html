How it works

Speak. Confirm. Share.

  1. Speak

    The doctor says the prescription in their own language. AI turns it into a clear draft.

    "Metformin 500, twice a day after food, 30 days."

  2. Confirm

    The doctor checks the draft. EK FILE flags allergies before anything is final.

    Amoxicillin? She is allergic to penicillin.

  3. Share

    The prescription prints with a QR code and goes to the patient on WhatsApp, with reminders.

    Next visit: 3 December.

Who it's for

Something for everyone in the visit

For doctors

  • No typing: speak the prescription
  • Allergies and history on one screen
  • Follow-ups that bring patients back

For patients

  • Prescription on WhatsApp or SMS
  • Medicine reminders in their language
  • Free, always, with no app to install

For families

  • Parents' and children's records on one phone
  • Replaces the "mummy-papa's reports" folder
  • Reminders reach the son or daughter too
