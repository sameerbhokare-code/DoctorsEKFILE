How it works

A clinic visit, step by step

EK FILE follows the steps a clinic already takes. Each one gets faster or goes digital, and nobody has to change how they work.

  1. Arrive

    Reception finds the patient by mobile number, or registers a new one in about 20 seconds.

  2. Summary

    The doctor sees allergies, current medicines and recent visits on one screen.

  3. Speak

    The doctor talks naturally in Marathi, Hindi or English. AI drafts the prescription and visit note.

  4. Confirm

    The doctor checks the draft, edits it if needed and confirms with one click.

  5. Print

    The prescription prints as usual, now with a QR code linking to the digital record.

  6. WhatsApp

    The patient or a family member gets the record with medicine and follow-up reminders.

  7. Next doctor

    The patient shares their history by OTP or QR, only if they choose to.

Noisy clinic? The doctor edits the draft on screen, and typing stays as a backup.No smartphone? SMS and the printed QR prescription still work.

The family health locker

Almost every Indian home has a “mummy-papa's reports” folder. EK FILE replaces it with one file per person, kept safe and shared only with consent.

Built on India's digital health system

India built the health ID. Small clinics still write on paper.

ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is India's 14-digit digital health ID under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. It doesn't store records. It links records held by hospitals, labs and clinics, shared only with the patient's consent. A clinic can link records only if it uses ABDM-enabled software. EK FILE is that software for small clinics.

ABHA is the road. EK FILE is the vehicle small clinics need to use it.

Where we stand: EK FILE is not yet integrated with ABHA. Integration needs development on ABDM's sandbox and certification, which is in our 12-month plan.

Questions doctors ask us

Before you try it

Does AI write the prescription?

AI writes a draft from what the doctor says. The doctor reads every line, edits it if needed and confirms it. Nothing prints or reaches the patient until the doctor confirms.

Do patients need a smartphone or an app?

No. Patients get their prescription and reminders on WhatsApp, or by SMS on a basic phone. The printed prescription with its QR code still works for patients who prefer paper.

What if the clinic's internet is slow?

EK FILE is designed for weak connections. The doctor can still type and print the prescription, and the record is sent once the connection returns.

Who can see a patient's record?

Only the clinic that treated the patient, and any other doctor the patient chooses to share it with by OTP or QR. Sharing is for a limited time, the patient can cancel it, and every access is logged. It is designed around India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

How is this different from Practo or Eka Care?

EK FILE is built for the single-doctor neighbourhood clinic. The doctor speaks in Marathi, Hindi or English instead of typing, patients need no app, and the paper prescription stays, with a QR link added.

What will it cost a clinic?

The pilot is free. After the pilot, clinics start on a free basic plan and pay a low monthly fee for advanced features. We will set the price with pilot doctors. Patients never pay.

Is it connected to ABHA today?

Not yet. ABHA integration needs development on ABDM's sandbox and certification, which is planned within our first 12 months.
